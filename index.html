PhD Candidate in Education at Cairo University, with more than fifteen years of experience in broadcasting, media, editing, translation, and communication. My research explores the relationship between education, citizenship, social development, and digital innovation — work that bridges the academic and the applied.

My master's research on Citizenship Education in African Societies was published by the Egyptian Ministry of Culture and is catalogued in the libraries of Stanford University and Yale University. I am committed to using knowledge, communication, and technology to support learning, inclusion, and positive social impact across communities and cultures.
